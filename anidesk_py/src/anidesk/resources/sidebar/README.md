# 内置侧栏插画

这三张原创插画通过内置 `image_gen` 工具生成，未使用 CLI/API 回退。PNG 原图直接复制到本目录，程序在显示时裁切和缩放；它们会随 EXE 打包，无需联网下载。

- 海风来信：`sea.png`
- 静夜星河：`stars.png`
- 樱花小径：`sakura.png`

## 最终生成提示词

### sea.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create one production-ready raster illustration for the narrow left sidebar of the AniDesk desktop anime tracker. Portrait 512x1536 (1:3). Scene: a peaceful sunny coastal railway platform, blue sea, cloud towers, a dark-blue-haired young adult woman in a light white blouse and navy skirt with a satchel, viewed from behind and slightly sideways, standing in the lower half, wind in hair, sea gulls, refined beautiful Japanese anime illustration, deep teal and pale sky blue. Artwork only, NO typography, NO letters, NO logo, NO frame, NO UI, NO collage, NO watermark. Original characters, no existing franchise. Composition must work when shown only 180px wide: readable central lower focal subject, generous atmospheric negative space in the upper 35%, uncluttered silhouettes and quiet color gradation toward pale sky at top (night sky for night scene). Entire picture filled with the illustrated scene, no white margin. All people are adults, everyday modest clothing. Polished, evocative anime-film background quality with careful lighting and detail. This image will be bundled as a selectable built-in sidebar wallpaper.

Upper 35% is quiet sky and atmospheric negative space; detailed scenery mainly in lower half. Entire canvas is one image. No text, letters, logos, border, UI, watermark, collage, or existing franchise characters. All people are adults in modest everyday clothes.
